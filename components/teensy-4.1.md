# Teensy 4.1

**Kind:** microcontroller  
**Qty on pendulum:** 1

600 MHz Cortex-M7 control brain. Hardware quadrature, SPI for magnetic encoders, and enough PWM/timers for a 1 kHz cart-pendulum loop. **3.3V logic, not 5V tolerant.**
