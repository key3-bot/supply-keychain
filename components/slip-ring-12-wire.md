# 12-wire capsule slip ring

**Kind:** slip-ring  
**Qty on pendulum:** 2 (shoulder + elbow)

Gold-plated rotating contacts so power and encoder signals can cross a spinning joint without the harness twisting off.

## Specs

- 12 circuits, 2A each, 240V max
- ~300 RPM rated
- 28 AWG color-coded leads
